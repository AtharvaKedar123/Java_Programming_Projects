# findLeapYears 📅

## 📌 Overview
Finds all leap years within a given range based on standard calendar rules.

---

## 🎯 Features
- Range-based filtering
- Accurate leap year validation

---

## 🧠 Concepts Used
- Conditional logic
- Iteration

---

## ⚙️ Workflow
1. Iterate through range
2. Apply leap year conditions:
   - Divisible by 4
   - Not divisible by 100 (unless divisible by 400)
3. Collect valid years

---

## ⏱ Complexity
- Time: O(n)

---

## 📍 Learning Outcome
- Applying real-world logical conditions
